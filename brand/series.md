## Attempting to Suck  (pillar 3, Wednesdays, voiceover with B-roll)
Failing out loud while starting over in New York at 26. Ends on her line, never "follow for more".

| Ep | Topic | Her material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Intro: from respected to starting over | Manager in Toronto, speeches, appreciated. Now: school, dating, friendships. "Nobody taught me how to fail." |
| 2 | School | Hasn't studied since 2018. Not her native language. Math was always triggering. Feels stupid after feeling so respected. Discipline after years of freedom. |
| 3 | Dating | Ended a 5-year relationship, the scariest thing she's done, but she chose herself. 6+ months stuck, waiting for something to happen. Doesn't believe in the apps. |
| 4 | Friendships | Met one really good friend fast. Back home and in Toronto she had many; here not a lot, and people already have their people. Not the first choice as the new person. From social butterfly to disappearing in large gatherings. |

More from the strategy's idea bank: making a friend at a coffee shop, going to an event alone, things I'm bad at in my first year, weekly "what flopped" updates.
