# Look

Source of truth: Kohav's design system, https://claude.ai/artifact/JBF1inT7AXwf8zde9qk7yg
(read its `project/README.md` and `project/tokens.json` if anything here looks out of date).

New York at golden hour: taxi yellow, burgundy and brown over warm, cinematic footage.
It should feel like a film still, not a template.

## Colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| taxi-yellow | #F6B511 | Hooks and captions on dark/busy footage, always with a 2-3px burgundy outline. The highlighted word. |
| burgundy | #6B1A2A | Text on light footage and cream cards. The outline around yellow. |
| brown | #5A3A28 | Caption boxes on busy footage (radius 8px), carousel and Story backgrounds. |
| espresso | #24160F | Body text on cream, dark quote cards. |
| cream | #F6ECDD | Carousel and quote card backgrounds. |
| tan | #C9A07A | Dividers and small details only. |

The one text rule: light footage -> burgundy text. Footage too dark for burgundy -> taxi yellow
with a thin burgundy outline. No white captions, no other colors. Never burgundy on brown or night footage.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Hook on the first frame, quote cards, titles: **Playfair Display Italic**, ~112px, a few words only.
- Captions on every video: **Montserrat ExtraBold**, ~68px, 2-4 words at a time, sentence case or lowercase.
- Never Playfair for running captions. No shouting all caps.

## Video frame (1080 x 1920)
Keep text below y=220, at least 400px above the bottom, and 140px clear of the right edge.
Captions: 2-4 words, synced to her voice, active word in taxi yellow (burgundy on light footage),
one gentle pop or fade per caption group.

## Covers and photos
A real photo (her face in warm light, her street, her coffee) with one word in Playfair Italic.
No icon covers, clip-art or designed frames that look generated.

## Grade
Warm highlights, slightly lifted blacks, soft contrast, natural skin.

## Her world (repeat in every reel so people say "that's so you")
- Signature background: her bed, the photos on the wall behind her, warm lamp glow. The city for special episodes.
- Format: talking head on that background, with B-roll popping in as small framed inserts beside her face.
- Prop: her matcha.
- Hobbies on camera: running, matcha, walking New York.
- Series device: Attempting to Suck opens with the countdown to Jan 1, 2027 ("N days left").
